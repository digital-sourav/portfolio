Project & Client Overview

The client is a well-established, comprehensive dental care practice located in DeForest, Wisconsin, serving the greater Madison metropolitan area. While they maintained a strong physical clinic and a loyal patient base for routine and advanced restorative care, their digital footprint had stagnated, limiting their ability to capture high-intent local search demand.

The Challenge

Despite the practice having a long-standing website and physical outlet, the domain suffered from negligible traffic and poor organic visibility. The site failed to rank for high-priority client keywords and was plagued by severe technical issues.

An initial audit revealed toxic indexation (such as "/thank-you/" and "/lp/*" pages appearing in SERPs), high load times, a weak backlink profile, and absolutely no presence in local search radiuses across DeForest and Madison.

Methodology

Strategic Execution

Technical SEO & Core Web Vitals

  • Reduced Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) from 9+ seconds by converting outdated PNG images to next-gen WebP formats using TinyPNG.
  • Eliminated Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) errors by explicitly defining image dimensions in WordPress custom CSS.
  • Fixed critical indexation and accessibility by updating robots.txt with the sitemap.xml and placing an HTML sitemap in the footer.
  • Implemented targeted Schema Markup, including Organization, Local Business (Dentist), Breadcrumbs, and FAQ Page schemas.

On-Page & Topical Authority

  • Resolved severe anchor text spam and keyword stuffing across legacy blogs.
  • Restructured URL architecture for immediate impact, changing paths from /services/service1.html to localized paths like /location/service1.html.
  • Developed a 3-month keyword strategy utilizing topical clusters and engaging blog formats (listicles, how-to guides) to drastically improve Time To Value.
  • Secured "People Also Ask" SERP features by integrating ACF-powered FAQ sections directly onto service pages.

Local SEO & Off-Page Authority

  • Overhauled the Google Business Profile (GBP) with geo-targeted images, service-specific updates, and linked social accounts.
  • Utilized Local Falcon to map and monitor localized grid visibility.
  • Acquired high-quality backlinks via digital PR and guest blogging, maintaining strict NAP consistency to ensure localized authority.
The Impact

Unlocking Raw Organic Potential

Within six months, the site transformed from a stagnant digital asset into a dominant local acquisition channel, capturing top positions in the Google Map Pack and AI Overviews.
